# Zoology

## Animal Metabolism (Life)

The Idea of the evolutionary process is realized with the genesis of the animal organism, unifying the Concept of Life with its objectivity. The animal's body is inwardly a syllogism or system of interdependent organs that are animated and coordinated by and for the animal's mind. 

The organ systems of the animal are defined and divided according to their purpose and contribution in sustaining the animal as living. They are: the sensory system, the motility system and the nutritive system.

The sensory system is inwardly divided into its organs that carry out the necessary functions for the animal to process affective stimuli in its environment. Skeletal organs protect the animal from its environment, sensory organs translate experiences into bodily signals and neural organs relay bodily signals to the mind for further discernment and activity.

The motility system is inwardly divided into organs that enable the animal to effect its environment. Muscular organs provide the animal with the means to coordinate its body, respiratory organs facilitate the exchange of gases and heat for metabolism and circulatory organs transport vital nutrients throughout the body.

The nutritive system is inwardly divided into the organs responsible for digestion and processing of nutrients required for the self-maintenence of the animal. Digestive organs break down ingested food into nutrients, hormonal organs purpose nutrients into hormones which regulate the growth and development of the body and reproductive organs reproduce the animal species in time as a kind.

## Animal Intellect (Cognition)

The cognition of the animal is differentiated into simple faculties that condition the instinctual capacities of the species. However, since these faculties are only conditions for the animal's particular instinct, the intellect of the animal is restricted to its environmental niche by necessity. Animals can sense objects, feel pleasure and pain care for their young, communicate with others of its kind and can even remember its past, but the animal's mind is itself subsumed by the processes of its body such that activity of pure thinking itself is rendered impossible.


## Living Rationality / Spirit (Absolute Idea)

It is the absolute purpose of the intellect, however, to free itself from all externality and mediation in order realize its substance as freedom itself. Thought ought to be aware of itself as thought because it is the very awareness of its being as thought. The very positing of Thought's distinction from Nature, however, reflectively implies a rational being that thinks and beholds its own thought, which is none other than the living rationality of humanity, or Spirit.
