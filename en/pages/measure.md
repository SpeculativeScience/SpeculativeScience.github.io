# Measure

## Specific Quantity (Linear Algebra)

```
Immediate Measure (Scalar)

Mediated Measure (Vector)

Rule (Unit Vector)

Specifying Measure (Row Vector)

Specified Measure (Column Vector)

Measure Relation (Matrix)
```

The Immediate Measure is the simple unity of Quality and Quantity, that is, the Ratio of Quantities is preserved in and through quantitative change. If you were to scale something by a factor of 3, the Ratio of Quantities would remain the same (e.g. if there is 1 of x and 2 of y, both are scaled by a factor of 3, the new quantities that are 3 of x and 6 of y  are still the same ratio or proportion as 1 of x and 2 of y). However, a scalar implies that there is something to be scaled, so Measure is now determined as a vector quantity of two quantities (such as x and y, like in the example above). But the scaling of a vector presupposes that the vector already has a magnitude prior to being scaled, which in turn indicates that there is a unit vector or Rule for measurement.

Once Measure is conceived of as a Rule, it is posited as external to what is being measured by the Rule. The act of measuring, then, is an act of comparison between the Measure and the Measured. This Rule is a covector (of unit magnitude) whereas what is measured by the Rule is a vector (of any magnitude). The covector measures the length of the vector and the vector is what is measured by the covector. But the covector is dual to the vector and vice versa, so each measures the other. The two Measures measuring each other are unified or constitute a Measure Relation. In terms of linear algebra, covectors and vectors are described by row and column vectors respectively, so the Measure Relation is a matrix of vectors and covectors.

## Real Measure (Abstract Algebra)

```
Combination of Measures (Linear Combination):

Series of Measures (Generating Set):

Elective Affinity (Group):

Nodal Line (Topological Group)

Substrate (Homotopy)

Totality of Measures (Category)
```

Specified Measure and Specifying Measure are brought in combination with each other. The Specified Measure is quantifying thing, like a graduated cylinder or thermometer, whereas the Specifying Measure is the thing quantified, like the volume of some amount of water or the temperature of a room. However, the choice of a standardized Specified Measure is arbitrary and conventional - you can quantitatively relate any two things as long as the two are qualitatively overlap, like having volume or temperature that can be calculated. Since the choice of Specified Measure is entirely at the discretion of the measurer, so what results a series of measures that runs on indefinitely, unable to be Specifying Measure for what it is (Measureless). However, the quantities between each series of measures have determinate qualitative relationships precisely because the relation of quantities themselves are qualitatively invariant. This is an Elective Affinity, wherein you have a group-structure that brings together relationships / symmetries between a series of measures (e.g. the four seasons form a cyclic group, acid-base interactions form a klein-four group, a deck of cards forms a permutation group, so do the roots of polynomials, etc.)

Elective Affinity displays a continuous progression from one affinity to another. It is a series of affinities that stands in contrast to the neutral line it occupies. To move from one affinity to the next (i.e. a group action of a group) is to make a quantitative leap across what is excluded by affinity. Hence, each affinity is a “node” on a neutral line. This change from one affinity to another is a change from one Measure Relation from one to another. These differences in affinity causes the Measure Relation to be self-repelling, forming a Nodal Line of Measure Relations, wherein each node denotes a change in Quality and each line denotes a change in Quantity.

## Becoming of Essence

```
Measureless ( Totality of Measures )

Absolute Indifference ⧁ Inverse Ratio of Factors 

Essence ⧁ Shine
```

The Nodes of the Nodal Line form a Totality of Measures since each Measure gets its character as a Measure by measuring and being measured by other Measures. But the Line of the Nodal Line is excluded from this totality because it is not a Measure. The Line is the very non-being of Measure, so it is Measureless. 

Each Measure implies another Measure in measuring its other, but the Measureless remains Indifferent to these changes across Measures. However, this Absolute Indifference subsumes the Totality of Measures within itself precisely *because* it is Indifferent and does not alternate or change into another Measure. This "non-immediacy" that subsumes and identifies Being is Essence.
