# Gravity

## Elasticity (Specific Quantum)

Gravity is the harmonic motion of the cosmos itself, wherein the motion of space is inert to its own movement as mass and the motion of mass is compliant to its own movement as space. The cosmos universally oscillates and interferes with itself at particular regions where its movement is inert or self-opposed, which shapes the mass of an individual celestial body. The formation of celestial bodies themselves attract and repel other bodies, thereby constituting itself as a system of many bodies, where the conditions of motion for each individual body are actualized.

## Orbital Motion (Real Measure)

A system of celestial bodies divides itself into a self-rotating solar center, moons that orbit the solar center but do not themselves rotate and planets which simultaneously rotate due to its moon(s) and orbit a solar center. 

Suns exhibit self-rotation because its momentum (internal motion) is primarily conserved as angular momentum, but it is highly inert to orbiting. Moons (satellites / comets) exhibit orbiting because their velocity (external motion) is already posited by a Sun which has solicited its motion, but is highly inert to rotating. Only an Earth is a free celestial body because it moves its Moon and is itself moved by its Sun.

## Vacuum Polarization (Indifference)

Space and matter are, in the first place, immediate and immanent to the cosmos, and gravity is their relative identity. But gravity shows itself to be measureless to the relative motions of celestial systems by cosmic expansion. However, even the measurelessness of cosmic expansion, though negating gravity measure, is itself a unity of space and matter. The immediacy of this unity is a self-annullment, the simplest substance of the cosmos which mirrors the whole within itself and so does not externally fall apart into space or matter. This non-immediate substance is Light.
