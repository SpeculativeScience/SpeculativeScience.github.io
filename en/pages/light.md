# Electromagnetism

## Light (Reflection)

Light is the indivisible substance into which Nature is factorized. As indivisible, Light is without parts, reflecting within itself what is outside of itself. Light images Space and Space is imaged by Light. The image posits what is imaged, but the imaged, in being reflected, reflects and posits its image. If Space is the immediacy of Nature, then Light is the mediation of Nature with its image. 

Therefore, the movement of light through space is nothing other than the density and compressibility of dark energy itself. Where space is compressed, it is positively charged and where space is stretched, it is negatively charged. This creates apparent inertial masses that cancel each other in their mutual opposition, giving light its zero rest energy. 

## Vortices (Ground)

The motion of quantum vortices are determined and constituted by pressure forces, where vortices of opposite spin attract and vortices of identical spin repel. In attraction, dark energy is absorbed and emitted away from each other, but in repulsion, dark energy is absorbed and emitted towards each other. This mutual self-grounding provides a physical explanation for Coulombic forces, but on the condition that the absorption of dark energy is counterbalanced by its emission. Therefore the mass of any charged vortex, formed by the confinement circularly-polarized light, remains constant and do not continuously increase with time. This outflow of dark energy is emission of virtual photons, which logically consistent with how electric charge is accounted for in quantum electrodynamics.

A charged vortex does not decay due the inflow and outflow of dark energy. By contrast, a neutral vortex can only absorb but cannot emit dark energy. This implies that neutral vortices are unstable in isolation and so would decay to its ground, which is the neutrino. This is the weak interaction.

Matter-antimatter anihilation is caused by disentanglement of vortices of opposing spin, which is what is observed in electron-positron annihilation. The bound-states of vortices, such as that of a proton, can be pictured as system of three interacting quarks, which are themselves vortices of complimentary spins. This is the strong interaction.


## Particle (Existence)

A vortex can only exist under the condition that it has the necessary energy to maintain its oscillation frequency. This necessary energy is self-confined light of the vortex itself, where the activity of light's oscillation is dissipated as heat. The dissipation of a vortex dissipates in absorbing the dissipated heat of its medium and absorbs its absorption in dissipating the absorbed heat of its medium. With this development, however, electrodynamics of light is superceded by the thermodynamics of particles.
