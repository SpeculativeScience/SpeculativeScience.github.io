# Biology

## Microbes (Mechanism)

The microbial organism immediately determines itself as objective and independent by internalizing the metabolism of the Earth for itself. The sentience of the microbe is the whole that animates the chemical processes constituting its parts to, thereby reproducing itself as living. However, microbial sentience has no capacity for mind beyond the coordination of nutrition, perceiving only whether something can be assimilated into itself or not. 

## Plants (Chemism)

The plant is explicitly differentiated from other organisms by its purposing of light for its own growth. Contrary to the microbe, whose metabolism is self-confined and reproduces only itself, the metabolism of the plant is external to itself and so dedicates everything to its vegetation, dividing into cells that are each capable of reproducing the whole plant via budding.

## Orthogenesis (Teleology)

The world of organisms, as a totality, are natural beings that reproduce themselves in space and time by interacting with each other and their environment, the Earth. Although organisms are internally purposive, this purpose can only be realized under the condition that the organism is adapted to other organisms and its environment. This shows the evolution of species to be a necessity, but a necessity that necessitates the evolution of purposes in and for the intellect of Nature.
