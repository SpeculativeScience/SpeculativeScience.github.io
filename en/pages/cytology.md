## Cell Morphology

The metabolic processes of the cell are directed towards the universal purpose of growth, to assimilate the chemical materials of its environment in order to produce new cells. But in constantly striving to divide itself into generations of new cells, the morphology, assimilation and replication are of the cell are all interdependent moments of the cell's existence. If we initially want to consider all of this only as the inner formation of the cell, the question arises as to which a priori the elements of this form must be, which we can then actually find in experience. Because the cell is still bound to space and is unable to free itself from it, then it is already clear from this that it cannot free itself from the formative elements of space. If the cell stands midway between the geological and animal organism, it will also have a mediating relationship to the configurations of space. For the Earth, the point, the plane, and the crystal appear as completely independent structures, lying peacefully beside and outside each other. In the animal, all these spatial forms are not only self-productions of the organism, but are also organized in the specific shape of organism. Even the cell implicitly contains the form of the animal, they do so only as differentiated cells of a self-differentiating form. Every cell is produced from another cell, but they only appear side by side in space and one after the other in time, so that the multiplicity of dividing cells eventually returns to a unity of multiplicity by forming an interrelated colony of cells, whereas the animal is always already a self-sustaining unity. This stage includes three components that provide the foundation for cell morphology: first, the nucleus as the potential cell, second, the organelles that carry out different functions of the cell, and third, the unity of organelle functions towards the universal purpose of cell division.

### Nucleus

The nucleus is the primary component of the cell in that it already contains the prototype of the entire cell that will be generated in the future. 

### Organelles

The functional activity of organelles depend on the production of proteins, and that itself depends on the messenger RNA communicated by the nucleus. However, the nucleus is only potentially the entire cell and has no reality on its own. It is because of this necessary connection of the nucleus to the organelles that the cell as a concept is realized.

## Cell Physiology

The external objects to which the cell relates are not yet individual forms, precisely because it is not yet an individual that is a self-contained unity. For this reason, it has no uninterrupted intussusception by which it could exclude itself from other individuals, so it relates in uninterrupted activity to the general elements of nature in order to absorb them.
