# Extension

Nature is the image of Reason. In thinking itself, Reason externalizes itself as the thought of itself and this self-external thought of Reason is Nature. 
By contrast, the self-internal thought of Reason would be Reason itself since each concept within Reason is a necessary condition for its self-generation as the unconditioned Truth. This active unity of self-externality and self-internality is Mind. 

## Space and Time (Pure Being)

The self-externality of Nature is Space. There is nothing beyond Space because Space is its own beyond. In positing a beyond, Space distinguishes itself from itself in the dimensionless point. What is external to the point is the non-point, or dimensionality. However, the point and non-point are distinctionless since the distinction between them is only external, but internally, each is without distinction. Dimensionlessness presupposes dimensionality, so the point extends beyond and into itself (another point) as a line and the line extends beyond and into itself (another line) as the surface. In being enclosed by its boundary, the surface extends beyond itself encloses itself and bounds its boundary. As self-enclosing, the surface that bounds its boundary is now itself the boundary for the volume that it encloses. The enclosed volume is undifferentiated Space that is self-external, so this enclosed Space posits a new beyond *within* itself as another dimensionless point, and so on. This process of recursion produced by spatial externalization is Time.

Space self-externalizes itself from itself, but in so doing, self-internalizes and iterates itself within itself. The identity of iterations is Space and the difference of iterations is Time. As such, whenever this Space vanishes into that Space, there is Time. The vanishing of that Space is the Future and the vanishing of this Space is the Past. The Future is what comes-to-be and the Past is what ceases-to-be, but each negates the other into Nothing (i.e. the past and future are intuitions cognized by memory and imagination, respectively). The negation of this process of negation is the Present, which is not this "now" (i.e. an event), but the *absolute* Present recollected as Eternity.

With the concept of Eternity, the passage of Time has passed over into timelessness. This is because a moment *in* Time is simultaneously a moment *in* Space, a Position. However, a Position can only be a moment in Time if this Position is *moving* to that Position. Therefore, the unity of Space and Time is Motion. 

## Volume and Frequency (Determinate Being)

Wherever there is Motion, there is an object that moves. Since motion is the unifying concept of Space and Time, the object in motion is spatiotemporal by definition. The object's quantity of space is its Volume and its quantity of time is its Frequency. The volume of the object its spatial extension and the frequency of the object is the rate at which it moves in time. 

## Energy (Being-for-Self)

Because the object has a volume, it occupies a definite Position in space at all times. But for the object to have a frequency, its preceding position must be negated and be directed towards its successive position. Each position is self-external to every other position, so the object persists in motion as conditioned by preceding and successive positions. Its motion does not vanish, however, if its preceding and successive positions are one and the same. This quantity of motion congealed within an object is its Energy. (i.e. the frequency of its volume)
