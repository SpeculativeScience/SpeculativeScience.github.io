# Geology

## Earth (Concept)

The Earth is the Concept of Nature in its reality. Its process of formation is immanently shaped as a universal body by gravity, is particularized into its metabolic cycles of volcanic, oceanic and atmospheric activity and is individual in being nothing more than its own animation and activity.

## Minerals (Judgment)

The Mineral is the Earth in its particularity, expressed as the interconnection of its different elements forming itself. In the first moment, the mineral is formed igneously from magma that has risen to the surface and cooled. In the second, the igneous mineral is subjected to chemical dissolution and precipitation in being eroded by the oceans and wind. In the third, the sedimentary mineral is returned to the Earth, compacted under tremendous heat and pressure until it is transformed via metamorphosis. The judgments of volcanic, oceanic and metaphorphic activity return to themselves in the syllogism of hydrothermal circulation, establishing the necessary conditions for cellular metabolism to take place.

## Cells (Syllogism)

The self-generating chemical reactions in hydrothermal vents yield a disequlibrium of hydrogen, carbon dioxide and pH, thereby lowering the kinetic barriers for protocell formation. With the formation of the cell, the metabolism of the Earth is inwardized in powering the construction of itself, as well as conditioning the possibility of reproducing itself as a kind.

From this standpoint, if the Earth is an organism, its hydrothermal vents can be understood to be its reproductive organs. No organism is eternally fertile, for the purpose of an egg is realized and over in its hatching.
