# Presuppositionlessness

In order to derive anything, you have to start somewhere. Every philosophy is faced with the choice of starting with an immediate or mediated beginning. Hegel’s chooses immediacy, but why does he make this choice? Every mediation is a logical result of a “given” prior immediacy, so to begin with mediation is to fail to begin since we have already presupposed what Reason is. The laws of logic, the “I”, sensare all examples of mediated beginnings because they already have distinctions and logical relationships internal to them. Immediacy is just immediacy, so by the meaning of the concept itself it is distinctionless and without any logical relationships.
	 
Each category following immediacy, then, is conditioned by the category preceding it and is the condition the category succeeding it. This syllogistic chain of categories does not go on indefinitely since the final category is the complete system of categories comprehended in its totality, so it has the first category (indeterminacy) included within it. The abstraction or projection of this first category out of the whole immanently generates the whole process, exactly how a flowering plant disperses a seed that grows over its lifetime into a plant that grows another seed and so on. Reason is purposive activity — it generates itself for itself by its own self-exposition because it is its own subject matter and method.
