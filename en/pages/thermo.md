# Thermodynamics

## Canonical Ensemble (World of Appearance)

The particle, as the appearance of matter, exists in such a way that its locality is suspended and is determined for the quantum potential. But the quantum potential embraces the particle's locality by conditioning it as one of its manifold properties. In this way, the particle is grounded by the quantum potential as its essence, but is at the same time the form in which quantum potential appears. The ground of the particle is no less an appearance than what it appears to be, and the locality of the particle is in this way mediated by the quantum potential, and is thus equally non-local. This inter-mediation is therefore a unity of connections with particles of the same kind, the development of its existence into a totality, which is its Canonical Ensemble.

## Conservation and Symmetry (Content and Form)

In a Canonical Ensemble, each particle is external to another. But the whole ensemble implies a total internal energy for its content, which is determined by the connection of particles with each other. In this way, the connection of the particle with itself is also determined by a symmetry of each force and with its respective displacement. A difference in temperature drives a change in entropy just as a difference in pressure drives a change in volume.

## Heat Engine (Essential Relation)

The interconversion of heat to electricity and electricity to heat are identical in content and find their immanently unity in the chemical element.
